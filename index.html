(04) The workshop

Slow is a
specification

One builder per instrument, start to finish. No subcontractors, no batch soldering, no instrument left open overnight.

An instrument takes eleven days of work. We spread them over five months so that nothing is done at the wrong temperature or in a hurry.

  • 01 No more than six instruments open at once.
  • 02 Panels etched in-house, in the darkroom at the back.
  • 03 Every unit runs 72 hours before it ships.

— Nuno, Inês & Rafa
the bench

  1. 01 — Panel

    Shear, etch, anneal

    Panels are sheared from 2 mm bone-anodised aluminium, etched in the darkroom and annealed flat. The legends are etched, not printed, so they will not rub off in ten years of patching.

    2 mm
    AL 5052
  2. 02 — Board

    Stuff & solder

    Through-hole, by hand, one builder per instrument. Each board is washed, inspected under the loupe and photographed before it goes into the case.

    Through-hole
    by hand
  3. 03 — Tune

    Trim to pitch

    Oscillators are trimmed to 1 V/Oct over six octaves at 23 °C. Filter tracking and envelope times are measured and written down by hand on your calibration sheet.

    1 V/Oct
    @ 23 °C
  4. 04 — Burn in

    72 hours, running

    Every instrument runs a recorded patch for three days with the lid on, in a room that gets warm in the afternoon. If something is going to drift, it drifts here — not in your case.

    72 h
    continuous
  5. 05 — Sign

    Number, sign, pack

    The serial is engraved, the builder signs the inside of the lid, and the case is packed with the calibration sheet, a patch book and four cables wound on the bench.

    Serial
    signed
